Methodology

Published formulas, weights, and the suppression rules

What this is (and is not)

The Citizen Index measures available public discourse — it is not a survey. Only ~20% of Nigerians are on social media (younger, more urban, more southern), and WhatsApp and radio cannot be accessed compliantly. We correct what we can and disclose what we cannot.

Record weight

w = a · s · r · (1 − b) — unique-author weight (down-weights power users), source reliability, recency decay, and a bot/coordination discount. Most bias correction is encoded here, in every number.

Data Confidence Score (the gate)

DCS = 100·(0.35·A + 0.20·D + 0.20·G + 0.15·I + 0.10·R) — sample adequacy, source diversity, geographic coverage, integrity, recency. Bands: ≥70 High, 55–70 Medium, 40–55 Low, <40 suppressed. A separate hard sample-size floor (≥25 unique authors) withholds tiny slices regardless of DCS. No number is published unless it clears this gate.

National Mood Index

NMI = 50 + 50·P, where P is the population-normalised weighted mean sentiment across states — so high-volume states and platforms cannot stand in for the country. Labels run Very positive → Highly distressed.

Complaint Severity

Blends volume, negativity, anger, geographic spread, velocity and source diversity, then applies a coordination risk discount. Coordinated content is excluded from the underlying figures, so a push campaign cannot buy a top spot.

Anger Index (computed, not yet published)

An Anger Index and leader scorecards are computed internally but withheld from public surfaces until an appeals-and-correction process and a validated track record are in place. Lawful, strongly-worded criticism is never labelled dangerous.

Model and taxonomy versions are recorded so any historical figure can be reproduced. Questions and corrections are welcome.
