About the data

What this can and can't tell you

We publish the gaps as plainly as the numbers. This index is strongest as a measure of direction and change over time, not an exact reading of national opinion.

  • This is an analysis of available public discourse, not a survey of all Nigerians.
  • Phase 1 is English-only; Nigerian Pidgin and Hausa are on the roadmap.
  • WhatsApp (Nigeria's most-used platform) and radio (dominant in the North) are not represented — they cannot be accessed compliantly.
  • Online discourse over-represents younger, urban, southern voices.

Full methodology →   Categories, sub-issues and problem-types are assigned by transparent classification; sentiment is measured per target; a Data Confidence Score gates every number and withholds under-powered slices.
